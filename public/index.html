Questions, answered.

Who can take part?

Any college student, undergraduate or postgraduate. Register with your college name and year, and pick the events you want to enter.

How much does it cost?

Each event entry is ₹300. If you plan to compete in several events, a Hero or Immortal pass costs less than entering one by one.

Can I enter more than one event?

Yes. Choose as many as you like on the registration form. Check the schedule first so the timings do not clash.

When and where is it?

The festival runs from 12 to 14 March 2027. Each event page lists its venue, and the full schedule is above.
